Research

Differential geometry + geometric analysis

Hidden structure in curvature.

I investigate how geometric assumptions constrain the local and global shape of Ricci solitons and almost solitons.

Ric+∇²f=λg

Research direction

Solitons, Weyl curvature, and warped products

A recurring question is when differential curvature conditions force a manifold to admit a more rigid description. In recent work, we study local multiply warped-product decompositions and bounds on the number of Ricci-tensor eigenvalues.

  • Ricci flow and geometric analysis
  • Gradient Ricci solitons
  • Harmonic Weyl curvature
  • Local structure and warped products
