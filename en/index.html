About this notebook

Geometry is where analysis, shape, and evolution meet.

I hold bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Mathematics from the University of Brasília (UnB), where I am currently pursuing a PhD. My research sits at the interface of differential geometry and geometric analysis.

Journal of Geometric Analysis 36, article 256 · 2026

Local structure of gradient almost Ricci solitons with harmonic Weyl tensor

Valter Borges · Matheus Andrade Ribeiro de Moura Horácio · João Paulo dos Santos

We study the local structure of gradient almost Ricci solitons with harmonic Weyl curvature and classify the case in which the Ricci tensor has exactly two distinct eigenvalues.

The Ricci tensor of a gradient Ricci soliton with harmonic Weyl tensor

Valter Borges · Matheus Andrade Ribeiro de Moura Horácio · João Paulo dos Santos

We give a shorter proof that the Ricci tensor of a gradient Ricci soliton with harmonic Weyl curvature has at most three distinct eigenvalues.
